Clipwave is operated by Christoph Alexander Siffling, Stuttgart, Germany ("we"). Contact: christoph@siffling.com.

What Clipfarm_BOB does

Clipfarm_BOB creates short highlight clips from Twitch streams of streamers who have agreed to take part, and uploads them to the operator's own TikTok account.

Data we process

  • Twitch chat activity: we only count the number of chat messages per second to detect highlights. Message texts and usernames are not stored.
  • Twitch clips: publicly available clips of participating streamers.
  • TikTok login: an access token and your TikTok Open ID, used only to publish videos to the connected account.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored on the operator's own server and are not shared with third parties. Clips are kept only as long as needed for upload and deleted afterwards. Published videos stay on TikTok, which has its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Streamers can ask at any time for their clips to be removed or for their channel to be excluded. You can also request access to, correction or deletion of your data, and revoke Clipwave's access in your TikTok settings. Write to [christoph@siffling.com].

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
